HCC Roster

HCC Roster, 7shifts style, with the 15 new features

Switch between the leader's Admin view and a volunteer's view. Whatever you do in one view shows up in the other: confirm a serve, swap, pick up an open spot, approve it, post an announcement. The 15 new features are listed on the right; switch any of them off under Admin, More, Features.

New in the roster

  • NEWPicker warnings away, doesn't do that service, already serving, serving a lot
  • NEWDraft tags Publish tells only the people whose spots changed
  • NEWSpot notes on the volunteer's card and serve details
  • NEWTemplates save a line-up and drop it on another date
  • NEWSwaps Requested, Accepted, Leader approved; the other volunteer accepts
  • NEWHands up on open spots leader sees everyone and picks one
  • NEWEvent banners Baptism Sunday on schedule, dashboard, cards
  • NEWVolunteer home serve today or day off, week with dots
  • NEWBadges counts on tabs
  • NEWAttendance Came, Late, No-show, Sick, Called off
  • NEWNotification settings publish skips people who turned messages off
  • NEWService checklists ticks show who and when
  • NEWAnnouncements by date to everyone serving one service
  • NEWOnboarding to-dos like the child-safety policy
  • NEWShout-outs a badge of thanks on Messages

Kept from 7shifts

  • KEPTHome with your next shift as "You serve Sunday" with Confirm or Can't make it
  • KEPTShift details with call time, who's serving with you, order of service, checklist
  • KEPTShift pool as Open spots with "I can serve"
  • KEPTTrade and cover as Swap and Give away, with the Requested, Accepted, Leader approved tracker
  • KEPTAvailability and time off as regular services and blockout dates
  • KEPTManager approvals in one Requests list
  • KEPTFind who's available when filling a spot, with blocked-out and already-serving shown
  • KEPTCopy week, templates, publish with notify
  • KEPTDashboard "who's working" as coverage per service
  • KEPTAnnouncements and group chats by schedule like "Serving Sunday AM"
  • KEPTOvertime warning as "serving too often"
  • KEPTLog book as Service notes with attendance
  • KEPTContacts, notifications, calendar sync

Skipped

  • SKIPClock in and out, timesheets nobody punches in at church
  • SKIPPay, tips, payroll volunteers aren't paid
  • SKIPSales vs labor charts
  • SKIPGeo-fence, photo check, health check
  • SKIPBreaks
